The house and its scent style

Lancôme is one of the big French perfume houses, and La Vie Est Belle is the bottle that took over the counter. It is a sweet iris gourmand: pear and flowers up top, praline and vanilla underneath. People who like dessert-leaning perfume often mean that one when they say Lancôme. The older names are still worth knowing. Trésor is the rose classic. Poême is a fuller floral with mimosa and vanilla. Idôle is the cleaner modern floral.

Those four cover most gift questions. La Vie Est Belle if they want sweet. Poême or Trésor if they want a fuller, more traditional floral. Idôle if they want something lighter and newer. This page checks two of them: La Vie Est Belle and Poême.

Best-known fragrances to look for

La Vie Est Belle

A Lancôme bottle with a checked price on this page. Floral gourmand: pear, iris, jasmine, and orange blossom over praline, vanilla, and tonka. The sweet Lancôme people already know.

Poême

The other Lancôme bottle with a checked price here. A fuller floral oriental: mimosa, jasmine, and tuberose with vanilla and tonka. Richer and more classic than La Vie Est Belle, less of a dessert.

Trésor

The rose Lancôme, a classic women's perfume that predates La Vie Est Belle. Softer and more traditionally floral than the gourmand. This page does not price it.

Idôle

The modern Lancôme floral, cleaner and less sweet than La Vie Est Belle. The name to look for when they want a current Lancôme that is not a praline perfume. Not priced here.

Which one to gift

Choose La Vie Est Belle when the person likes sweet perfume, iris, and a praline dryness under the sugar. It is one of the easier famous gifts because so many people already know the smell. Choose Poême when they like a fuller, more traditional floral and would find La Vie Est Belle too dessert-like. Mimosa, jasmine, and tuberose make Poême the dressier, older-style Lancôme.

Trésor is the rose to look for if neither of those fits, and Idôle is the lighter modern one. This page prices La Vie Est Belle and Poême only. A side-by-side with Valentino Donna Born in Roma is linked below when you are choosing between two sweet florals.

FAQ

What does La Vie Est Belle smell like?

Pear, iris, jasmine, and orange blossom, with praline, vanilla, and tonka. A sweet floral gourmand. The checked bottle is in the grid.

How is Poême different from La Vie Est Belle?

Poême is a fuller floral: mimosa, jasmine, tuberose, vanilla, and tonka. La Vie Est Belle is sweeter and more gourmand, with pear, iris, and praline. Both are priced on this page. They are not the same perfume.

Is Trésor the same as La Vie Est Belle?

Trésor is the classic rose Lancôme. La Vie Est Belle is the sweet iris-and-praline perfume. People who grew up with Trésor are not automatically asking for La Vie Est Belle.

Which Lancôme is the safer gift?

La Vie Est Belle is the safer famous gift for someone who likes sweet perfume. Poême is the better gift for someone who likes a rich floral and finds dessert perfumes too sweet. The grid has the checked prices for both.
