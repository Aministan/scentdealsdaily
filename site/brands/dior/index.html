The house and its scent style

Dior fragrances cover both the counter classics and the newer men's blockbusters. Sauvage is the men's cologne most people mean now: bergamot and pepper over a woody dry-down. Sauvage Elixir is the thicker parfum version, not a bigger bottle of the eau de toilette. Eau Sauvage is the older citrus cologne, from 1966, and it is a different smell from Sauvage.

On the women's side, J'adore is the floral people already know, built around ylang-ylang, jasmine, and rose. Miss Dior is a separate perfume. The grid only prices a bottle after we have checked it.

Best-known fragrances to look for

Only the bottles in the grid have a price we checked. The other fragrances below are here so you can tell them apart. This page does not assign them a price.

Sauvage

The men's Dior with bergamot, pepper, and ambroxan. The eau de parfum is fuller than the eau de toilette. It is in the grid when that bottle is checked.

Sauvage Elixir

The concentrated Sauvage: spice, lavender, and woods, heavier than the eau de parfum. A different bottle, and it is in the grid when the check includes it.

Eau Sauvage

The classic citrus cologne, lemon and herbs, not the 2015 Sauvage. People mix the names up. It is in the grid when that bottle is checked.

J'adore

The women's floral: ylang-ylang, jasmine, and rose. It is not Sauvage and it is not Miss Dior. It is in the grid when the check includes it.

Which one to gift

Give Sauvage to someone who wants the modern men's Dior. Give Sauvage Elixir only if they want that smell turned thicker and spicier. Give Eau Sauvage to someone who asked for the older citrus cologne, not for Sauvage.

J'adore is the women's gift when they want a floral Dior. Do not substitute it for a men's Sauvage bottle. Buy the name that is in the grid.

FAQ

Is Sauvage the same as Eau Sauvage?

No. Eau Sauvage is the older citrus cologne. Sauvage is the later men's cologne built on bergamot and pepper. The grid names the one we checked.

Is Sauvage Elixir the same bottle as Sauvage?

Elixir is the thicker, spicier parfum version. It is not a larger eau de toilette of Sauvage. Buy the concentration named in the grid.

What does J'adore smell like?

A floral perfume: ylang-ylang, jasmine, and rose. It is the women's Dior, not a flanker of Sauvage. The checked bottle is the one in the grid.
