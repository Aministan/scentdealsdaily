The house and its scent style

Jean Paul Gaultier fragrances are playful on purpose. The bottles are costumes, and the smells are just as direct. Le Male, in the sailor torso, is the men's classic: mint and vanilla, sweet and fresh at the same time. Classique is the women's vanilla and orange blossom in the corset bottle. Scandal is the sweeter women's perfume people buy when they want something louder. Ultra Male pushes Le Male further into sweet territory.

Le Beau is the tropical one. Coconut and tonka, still clearly Gaultier, less minty than Le Male. That is the bottle we checked. If someone says they want the original JPG men's cologne, they almost always mean Le Male, not Le Beau.
