The house and its scent style

Valentino's current fragrance reputation sits on Born in Roma. Donna Born in Roma is the women's perfume: jasmine and bourbon vanilla, a sweet floral that became a default gift. Uomo Born in Roma is the men's line, and it comes in more than one concentration. The eau de toilette is fresher and mineral. The Intense is sweeter, with vanilla and lavender. Voce Viva is the other women's Valentino people still ask about, a separate floral from Donna.

All three Born in Roma bottles that shoppers mix up are on this page: Donna, Uomo, and Uomo Intense. The gift gets easy once you sort women versus men, and fresh versus sweet.

Which one to gift

Donna Born in Roma is the women's gift when they like jasmine and vanilla together. It is in the same shopping conversation as other sweet florals, including Lancôme La Vie Est Belle, and it is still its own perfume. Uomo Born in Roma eau de toilette is the men's gift when they like fresh, slightly mineral cologne. Uomo Intense is the men's gift when they like vanilla and want the sweeter Born in Roma.

The usual mistake is buying Intense for someone who wears the fresh Uomo, or buying Uomo for someone who wanted Donna. Read the gender and the word Intense before you use the grid. Voce Viva is the other women's name, and it is not priced here.

FAQ

What does Valentino Donna Born in Roma smell like?

Black currant, jasmine, bourbon vanilla, and cashmeran. A sweet floral. The checked bottle is in the grid.

What is the difference between Uomo Born in Roma and Intense?

The eau de toilette is mineral and fresh: salt, violet leaf, ginger, sage, and vetiver. Intense is vanilla, lavender, and vetiver, sweeter and heavier. Both are priced on this page.

Which Valentino should I gift?

Donna Born in Roma for a woman who likes jasmine and vanilla. Uomo eau de toilette for a man who likes fresh cologne. Uomo Intense for a man who likes sweet vanilla. All three have checked prices in the grid.

Is Voce Viva the same as Donna Born in Roma?

Voce Viva is a separate women's Valentino floral. Donna Born in Roma is the jasmine-and-vanilla Born in Roma perfume. This page prices Donna, Uomo, and Uomo Intense.
