The house and its scent style

Elizabeth Arden has a long fragrance counter, and two names still do most of the work. Green Tea is the light one: citrus and tea, the bottle people wear to work and in warm weather because it stays fresh and does not fill a room. Red Door is the classic floral, richer and more dressed up. Sunflowers is the cheerful daytime floral, easier and brighter than Red Door.

The Green Tea line has many flankers. The one to start with is the original green-tea scent, which is what we checked. 5th Avenue is another women's Arden, a softer floral, and it is a third path if neither tea nor Red Door sounds right. The Green Tea name covers a lot of limited versions. The bottle in the grid is the original citrus-tea scent. A flanker with a fruit or flower in the name is a different product.

Which one to gift

Green Tea is the Elizabeth Arden gift for someone who likes light, fresh perfume and dislikes heavy sweetness. It suits warm weather, offices, and a recipient who says most perfume gives them a headache. It is a weak match for someone who wants a romantic floral or a vanilla night scent. Those shoppers are closer to Red Door or Sunflowers.

If they already own a Green Tea flanker, check the name on the bottle before you assume the original is what they want. The only Elizabeth Arden price on this page is the Green Tea bottle in the grid.

FAQ

What does Elizabeth Arden Green Tea smell like?

Lemon, bergamot, rhubarb, green tea, jasmine, and oakmoss. A light citrus-tea perfume for daytime. The checked bottle is the one in the grid.

Is Green Tea the same as Red Door?

Green Tea is a fresh citrus-tea scent. Red Door is the classic, richer floral. They are both Elizabeth Arden, and they suit different people.

Is Green Tea a good office perfume?

It is one of the lighter mainstream perfumes, which is why people wear it to work and in the daytime. It is a poor choice when the gift is supposed to be a heavy evening scent.

Do you price Red Door or Sunflowers?

This page prices the Elizabeth Arden bottle in the grid, which is Green Tea. Red Door and Sunflowers are described so you can tell them from Green Tea, and they are not given a price here.
