Deals by brand

How to use this list

This is the brand index for ScentDealsDaily. Each name opens that house: the bottles we price-checked at Perfumania, FragranceShop, or FragranceNet, with the size and the compare-at price that retailer listed. A brand page also explains how the house tends to smell, which famous bottles people ask for, and which checked bottle fits a gift. We do not fill in a discount, a stock status, or a star rating that is not part of that check.

The houses run from fresh daytime colognes to sweet gift perfumes and a few higher-priced bottles. If you already know the brand, open it. If you are shopping by person instead of by logo, the holiday gift guides group checked bottles for him, for her, and for specific recipients. Men's colognes, women's perfumes, and unisex bottles have their own lists, and the roundups below group bottles we already checked by style.

A brand page only shows a price for a bottle in its grid. Other well-known fragrances are named so you can tell whether the bottle on sale is the one you meant: Terre d'Hermès versus H24, CK One versus Eternity, Eros versus Bright Crystal. Side-by-side comparisons and scent roundups are linked from a brand page only when that page's bottles are already on them. The date under the heading is the check behind the numbers in the grid.
