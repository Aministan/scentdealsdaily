FAQ

What does Dolce & Gabbana Light Blue smell like?

A fresh women's eau de toilette: apple, Sicilian lemon, cedar, and white rose. It is a daytime perfume. The checked bottle is the one in the grid.

Is Light Blue for men the same bottle?

No. Light Blue for men is a separate cologne. The bottle on this page is the women's eau de toilette.

Is Light Blue Eau Intense the same as Light Blue?

Eau Intense is a flanker, fuller than the original. The grid prices the original women's eau de toilette, not the intense flanker, unless the name in the grid says otherwise.

Who should skip Light Blue?

Someone who wants a sweet vanilla, a heavy evening perfume, or the men's cologne. Light Blue is for a person who likes fresh citrus.
