How to choose

Split the list by the retailer note line. Chrome Legend is green apple and tea. Burberry Weekend is lemon, grapefruit, and mandarin. Boss Bottled Unlimited is mint, grapefruit, and sandalwood. Versace Pour Homme is lemon, bergamot, and neroli. Bvlgari Man Wood Neroli is neroli, bergamot, and cedar. Montblanc Legend Spirit is grapefruit, lavender, and white woods. Acqua di Gio is lime, bergamot, and marine notes. Those are the fresh daytime bottles. CK Free is juniper, coffee, and Virginia cedar, the woody aromatic. When you have never smelled what he wears, Weekend, Pour Homme, or Acqua di Gio is the lower-risk daily gift. CK Free fits when you already know he likes a woodier scent. The note line is the retailer's. This is not a quality ranking.

Use the stickers as a budget ladder, then read the size on the card. CK Free is 3.4 oz. Chrome Legend is 4.2 oz. Weekend and Legend Spirit are 1.7 oz. Bottled Unlimited is 3.3 oz. Pour Homme is the 1.0 oz bottle, so a mid sticker can still be a small amount of juice. Wood Neroli and Acqua di Gio are 3.4 oz.
