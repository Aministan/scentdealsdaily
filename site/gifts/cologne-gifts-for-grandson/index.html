How to choose

Split the list by the retailer note line. CK Free is juniper, coffee, and Virginia cedar. Chrome Legend is green apple and tea. Burberry Weekend is lemon, grapefruit, and mandarin. Boss In Motion is orange, cardamom, and cinnamon. Those are the daytime bottles. The Most Wanted Parfum is ginger, woods, and bourbon vanilla. Stronger With You Intensely is toffee, cinnamon, and vanilla. Uomo Born in Roma Intense is vanilla, lavender, and vetiver. Those are the sweeter bottles. When you have never smelled what he wears, Chrome Legend or Weekend is the lower-risk daily gift. Most Wanted or Stronger With You Intensely fits when you already know he likes a sweeter scent. Use the stickers as a budget ladder, then read the size on the card. CK Free and Uomo Born in Roma Intense are 3.4 oz. Chrome Legend is 4.2 oz. Boss In Motion is 3.0 oz. Burberry Weekend, The Most Wanted, and Stronger With You Intensely are 1.7 oz, so a mid sticker can still be a small amount of juice.
