What is the difference between Versace Eros Flame Cologne and Azzaro The Most Wanted Parfum Cologne?

Eros Flame opens bright with lemon, mandarin, black pepper and rosemary, then settles into rose, tonka bean, vanilla and sandalwood. The Most Wanted Parfum skips the citrus: ginger alone up top, a woody middle and a bourbon vanilla base, at parfum concentration, so it's denser and darker from the first spray.

Is Versace Eros Flame Cologne or Azzaro The Most Wanted Parfum Cologne stronger?

Versace Eros Flame Cologne is an Eau de Parfum and Azzaro The Most Wanted Parfum Cologne is a Parfum. Parfum has a higher concentration of fragrance oils than eau de parfum, so Azzaro The Most Wanted Parfum Cologne usually lasts longer and projects more, though skin chemistry matters.
