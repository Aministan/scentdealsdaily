FAQ

Which is cheaper, Montblanc Legend Spirit Cologne or Giorgio Armani Acqua Di Gio Cologne?

As of Oct 31, 2026 10:34 AM ET, Montblanc Legend Spirit Cologne is the lower price at $61.95 for 1.7 oz (Perfumania). Per ounce, Giorgio Armani Acqua Di Gio Cologne is the better value at $19.69/oz.

What is the difference between Montblanc Legend Spirit Cologne and Giorgio Armani Acqua Di Gio Cologne?

Legend Spirit opens with pink peppercorn, grapefruit and bergamot, moves to lavender, cardamom and an aquatic accord, and settles on white woods, oakmoss and white musks, per Montblanc. Acqua di Gio opens on lime, lemon, bergamot and orange with neroli, has a marine heart with rosemary, jasmine and nutmeg, and dries down to white musk, cedar, oakmoss, patchouli and amber, so it is more citrus-forward and a little warmer at the end.

Is Montblanc Legend Spirit Cologne or Giorgio Armani Acqua Di Gio Cologne stronger?

Both are sold as Eau de Toilette, so concentration alone doesn't separate them; performance depends on the composition and your skin.
