What makes a cologne 'fresh'?

Fresh colognes lead with citrus (bergamot, lemon, grapefruit, neroli), aquatic or green notes (tea, mint, green apple) instead of vanilla, amber or heavy spice. They read clean and light, which is why they're the usual pick for warm weather, the gym and the office.

Eau de toilette or eau de parfum for a fresh scent?

Most fresh colognes are eau de toilettes, which are lighter and easier to wear in heat. An eau de parfum (like Bvlgari Man Wood Neroli or Xerjoff Casamorati Mefisto here) has more fragrance oil, so it usually lasts longer on skin.
