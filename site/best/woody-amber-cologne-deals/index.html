What counts as a woody or amber cologne?

Woody colognes name cedar, sandalwood, vetiver, oak or a woodsy accord in the heart or base. Amber and amberwood add a warmer, resinous side. A fresh citrus that only mentions musk does not qualify, and a bottle whose retailer description disagrees with the published pyramid is left off.

Are woody colognes better in cold weather?

Cedar, sandalwood and amber usually show up more in fall and winter, when heat does not flatten them. The citrus-woody bottles here (Bvlgari Man Wood Neroli and Mefisto) are the easier warm-weather wears. The drier cedar ones, such as CK Free, suit cooler air.
