Are vanilla fragrances good for fall and winter?

Yes. Vanilla, tonka, toffee and praline feel warm and hold up well in cold air, so sweet scents like these are the classic fall, winter and date-night picks. In summer heat they can feel heavy, so use a lighter hand.

What does gourmand mean in perfume?

A gourmand fragrance is built around edible-smelling notes such as vanilla, caramel, praline, toffee, cacao or coffee. Vanilla is the most common; pairing it with florals (La Vie Est Belle, Donna Born in Roma) or lavender and spice (Born in Roma Intense, Stronger With You Intensely) changes how sweet it reads.
