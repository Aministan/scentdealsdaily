What makes a perfume fresh?

Fresh perfumes open on citrus (lemon, grapefruit, bergamot, mandarin), green tea or ozonic notes instead of vanilla, amber or a dense white floral. They stay light, which is why they are the usual pick for daytime, the office and warm weather.
